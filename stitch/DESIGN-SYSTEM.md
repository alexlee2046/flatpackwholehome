## MODULIV — Homepage (Desktop)

## Brand & Style

The design system is rooted in the "Japandi" philosophy—a fusion of Japanese functionalism and Scandinavian minimalism. It targets a sophisticated, design-conscious audience seeking order, warmth, and high-end craftsmanship. The emotional response is one of tranquility, precision, and tactile quality.

The style is **Warm Minimalism** with an **Editorial** lens. It prioritizes heavy whitespace to evoke a sense of air and luxury, allowing product photography to serve as the primary visual driver. The UI avoids unnecessary ornamentation, relying instead on masterful typography and a restrained, earth-toned palette to communicate premium value.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is governed by a **fixed-width grid** on desktop (1440px max-width) and a **fluid grid** on mobile. The structure is inspired by *Shoji* screens—clean, rhythmic, and balanced.

- **Grid:** 12-column system for desktop, 4-column for mobile.
- **Rhythm:** All vertical spacing should be a multiple of 8px.
- **Whitespace:** High-end appeal is achieved through "wasteful" whitespace. Content sections should have a minimum `section-gap` of 120px on desktop to allow the eye to rest between product categories.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a minimalist aesthetic, depth is communicated through **tonal layers** and **subtle ambient shadows** rather than heavy gradients.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Warm Porcelain (#F9F8F6).
- **Level 1 (Cards/Floating):** White (#FFFFFF) with a very soft, diffused shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(26, 28, 29, 0.04)).
- **Level 2 (Modals):** White (#FFFFFF) with a more defined shadow and a backdrop blur (8px) on the underlying content to create a focus state.
- **Outlines:** Use 1px solid borders in a slightly darker tint of the background (#EBEAE8) for structural definition in data-heavy areas like checkout.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
  - Primary: Solid Basalt Charcoal (#1A1C1D) with White text. No border.
  - Secondary: Outlined 1px Basalt Charcoal with transparent background.
  - Tertiary: Text only, 14px uppercase with a 1px underline that expands on hover.
- **Input Fields:** 
  - Minimalist design. Only a bottom border (1px) in Neutral-light, which turns to Primary (Terracotta) on focus.
- **Product Cards:** 
  - Borderless with a Level 1 shadow on hover. Images should be high-resolution, featuring natural wood textures and soft lighting.
- **Chips/Badges:** 
  - Small, pill-shaped with 12px Plus Jakarta Sans. Use Soft Sage (#545C50) for "In Stock" or "Sustainably Sourced" labels.
- **Progress Indicators:** 
  - Used in the "Flat-pack Assembly" guides. Thin 2px lines in Terracotta to denote step completion.
- **Navigation:** 
  - Top-aligned, centered logo, with menu items in Label-md. Subtle fade-in animations on hover.

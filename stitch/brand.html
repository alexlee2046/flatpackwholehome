01 · LOGO SYSTEM

Clear Space & Minimum Sizing Rules

Exclusion Zone (X-Height)

Always maintain a boundary of clear space around the wordmark equal to the height of the capital letter ‘T’ in ‘The’. No UI elements, imagery or trim cuts may encroach into this exclusion zone.

Digital Minimum Size

Wordmark: Min height 18px (width ~115px).
Six Slabs Icon: Min 20px on screen. At 16px (favicon scale), always utilize the Burnt Oak solid squircle background container.

Print Minimum Size

Wordmark: Min width 30 mm for flexographic or offset printing.
Carton Stamp: Min 40 × 40 mm on corrugated cardboard to prevent ink bleed across flutes.

05 · INTEGRITY RULES

Brand Integrity: Do’s and Don’ts

Consistency is the cornerstone of premium DTC brand equity. The following rules protect the dignity and legal standing of The Flat Set identity across all media.

check_circle

Approved Application (DO)

  • ✓ Always retain “The”: The legal registered trademark is The Flat Set. Never omit the definite article.
  • ✓ Keep Playfair Italic on “The”: Ensure the elegant italic slant is preserved at 55–60% of the main glyph cap-height.
  • ✓ Use Basalt on Porcelain: Highest contrast editorial presentation (#1A1C1D on #F9F8F6).
  • ✓ Keep the 6-slab icon proportions: Always 6 horizontal bars, with the bottom bar slightly indented (~8%).
cancel

Prohibited Treatment (DON’T)

  • ✕ Do not truncate: Never write “FlatSet”, “FLAT SET”, or “Flatset”.
  • ✕ Do not color the wordmark Terracotta: Terracotta (#A85F3B) is reserved strictly for promo ribbons, badges and accents, never as main logo ink.
  • ✕ Do not add gradients or drop shadows: The identity celebrates flat architectural honesty. Never apply bevels, gradients or 3D extrusions.
  • ✕ Do not alter slab count: The icon must strictly have 6 slabs. Do not reduce to 3, 4, or 5 lines under any circumstance.

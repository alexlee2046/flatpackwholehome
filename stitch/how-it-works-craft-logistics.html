Donation-Over-Return Policy

Returning large furniture creates massive carbon footprints. Re-compressing used foam is nearly impossible outside a factory.

Instead of shipping items back to a landfill, our ESG policy mandates a Donation-Over-Return approach. If you aren't satisfied, we arrange a pickup with local charities like Habitat for Humanity, and issue your full refund upon donation receipt. Zero waste. Maximum impact.

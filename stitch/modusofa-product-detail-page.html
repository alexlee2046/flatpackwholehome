LIVING WITH IT

Care Guide

Cream Bouclé arrow_downward

Nubby, cloud-like loops that hide everyday life. Vacuum monthly with a brush head; spot clean spills immediately with a damp white cloth. For a deeper clean, zip off the covers and hand wash cold. Keep out of direct sun to preserve the ivory tone.

Caramel Corduroy arrow_downward

Wide-wale corduroy that wears in like a favourite jacket. Zip off the covers, machine wash cold on gentle, line dry, and brush the pile back with the included oak brush once dry. Rotating the seat cushions monthly keeps the ridges even.

Olive Chenille arrow_downward

A low-sheen, moss-green weave that absorbs light and noise. Machine wash covers cold on gentle, line dry. Blot spills — don’t rub. The pile relaxes with use; a pass with a clothes steamer lifts it back.

Tech Grey arrow_downward

Our tightest, toughest weave — rated for pets, kids, and real weeks. Most spills wipe off with a damp cloth; covers machine wash warm and tumble dry low. This is the fabric we recommend to anyone who “has a dog.”

Oak frame & legs arrow_downward

Finished with a water-based matte lacquer — no polishing, ever. Wipe with a dry microfibre cloth; avoid silicone polishes. Oak darkens slightly over the first year. That’s not wear. That’s patina.
